**Milestone:** 2 — Advanced Form Controls (v1.1)

## Executive Summary

AutoFiller Milestone 2 (v1.1) has achieved its primary goal: expanding auto-filling capabilities beyond standard text inputs to encompass complex Google Forms controls—**dropdown menus, single-select radio button groups, multi-select checkboxes, and date pickers**—via native in-browser synthetic DOM event simulation.

All 3 planned engineering phases (Phases 12, 13, and 14) are **completed**, verified, and backed by a comprehensive unit and integration test suite with **160/160 tests passing** across `@autofiller/extension` (88 tests) and `@autofiller/backend` (72 tests). Production builds across all three monorepo workspaces (`shared`, `extension`, `backend`) compile cleanly without warnings or errors.

To accelerate delivering advanced form-filling capabilities to end-users, persona multi-profile switching (formerly Phases 15–17) has been deferred to Milestone 3 (v1.2).

## 3. Key Accomplishments in Milestone 2 (v1.1)

1. **Rich Form Control Recognition**: Extended DOM reader to inspect Google Forms compound components, extracting choices from dropdowns, radio groups, checkboxes, and date inputs.
2. **Constrained Prompting & Array Parsing**: Enhanced LLM prompt builder to enforce choice selection from known option sets and handle array-valued selections for multi-choice fields.
3. **In-Browser Synthetic Event Dispatcher**: Engineered 6 dedicated fill handlers supporting native and ARIA-based controls without external dependencies (Puppeteer/Playwright).
4. **Genuine Dropdown Selection Engine**: Overcame Google Forms dynamic popover rendering via coordinate-aware clicks, hover states, and reactive settlement.
5. **CSP-Safe Standalone Content Script**: Resolved strict Google Forms Content Security Policy blockers by building standalone IIFE content scripts (`.iife.ts`) with dynamic background injection fallbacks.
